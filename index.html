Primary Gear

DJI Air 3

I capture all my stunning aerial visuals using the powerful DJI Air 3. Equipped with a dual-camera system (wide-angle and 3x medium telephoto), this drone delivers breathtaking 4K/60fps HDR videos and 48MP photos.

Its advanced Omnidirectional Obstacle Sensing allows for incredibly safe and dynamic flights, enabling me to focus purely on capturing the perfect, cinematic shot for your projects in any environment.
